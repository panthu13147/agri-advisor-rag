• Complete the plantation of all fruit crops in the area with sufficient monsoon rains.
• In ambia bahar mandarin and sweet orange orchards, damaged twigs from sudden rain/thunderstorm be
removed and cut ends be smeared with Bordeaux paste (1 kg lime + 1 kg CuSo4 + 10 liter water).
Fruit crops Plantation • During this month there is rapid spread of citrus canker in acid lime (lemon) hence the affected leaves, branches
should be pruned and burnt. Spray copper oxychloride180 gm with streptocycline 6 gm in 60 liters of water
before the onset of the monsoon. Repeat the spray 3-4 times at an interval of 30 days.
Trans • Before transplanting chilly seedlings, dip the tops of the seedlings in a solution of 10 ml of Dimethoate 30% + 3 g
Vegetable plantation of water soluble sulphur (80%) + 2.5 g of Mancozeb in one liter of water.
/ Nursery • Before transplanting brinjal seedlings, dip the tops of the seedlings in a solution of 10 ml of 30% dimethoate per
10 liters of water.

management of livestock during rainy season.
