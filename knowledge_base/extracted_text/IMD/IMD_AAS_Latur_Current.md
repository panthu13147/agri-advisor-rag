Name of Crop Stage Agromet Advisory
Soybean Growth Intercultural operation should be done for weed management in soybean crop. If infestation of leaf eating catter piller & stem
borer may observed in soybean crop, For management take a spray of any one chemical as given below Profenofos 50% @ 400
ml or Ethion 50% @ 600 ml or Thimethoxam 12.6% + Lambda Cyhalothrin 9.5% @ 50 ml or if incidence is more take a spray
of Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% @ 60 ml per acre during clear weather condition. If yellow leaf observed in soybean crop due to
micronutrient deficiency for management take a spray of EDTA Chilated mix Micronutrients grade-2 @ 50 g or 50 ml with
19:19:19 @ 100 g per 10 liter of water during clear weather condition. If infestatin of yellow mosaic observed in soybean crop
for management, Remove and destroy yellow mosaic infected plants.
Kharif sorghum Growth Intercultural operation should be done for weed management in kharif sorghum crop. For management of fall armyworm in
kharif sorghumcrop take a spray of Emamectine Benzoate 5 % @ 4 g or Spinetoram 11.7 SC @ 4 ml per 10 liter of water during
clear weather condition.
Pearl millet Growth Light hoeing should be done in pearl millet crop for weed management and for soil moisture conservation. Light hoeing should
be done in soybean crop for weed management and for soil moisture conservation.
Sugarcane Vegetative Due to cloudy weather, an infestation of the 'Payrila' (moth) pest is being observed in the sugarcane crop. To monitor the pest
Growth and to trap and destroy large numbers of adult moths, a light trap should be installed at a height of six inches above the crop
canopy—at a ratio of one trap per five acres—and operated between 6:00 PM and 10:00 PM. For management, take a spray of
biological fungus Metarhizium anisopliae @ 40 grams in 10 liters of water or take a spray a 5% NSKE of for chemical control,
take a spray Chlorpyrifos 20% @ 600 ml or Monocrotophos 36% @ 200 ml of per acre during clear weather condition.
Turmeric Growth For management of Rhizome fly in turmeric take a spray of Quinalphos 25 % @ 20 ml or Diamethoate 30 % @ 10 ml per 10
liter of water with an interval of 15 days during clear weather condition. (As label claim was not given by Central Insecticides
Board for Rhizome fly in turmeric the research recommendation of university is mentioned here). Intercultural operations
should be done for weed management in turmeric crop during wapsa condition.
Citrus Growth As the *Mrig Bahar* orange and sweet lime orchards are currently in the flowering & Fruit Initiation stage, do not spray any
insecticide in Mrug bahar citrus orchard. Intercultural operations should be done for weed management in citrus orchard.
Pomegranate Growth As the *Mrig Bahar* pomegranate orchards Given the possibility of a disease outbreak in the orchard, spray a 1% Bordeaux
mixture during clear weather condition. Intercultural operations should be done for weed management in pomegranate
orchard.
Sapota Growth Intercultural operations should be done for weed management in Sapota orchard.
Vegetable Transplanting/ If sufficient moisture is available in soil sowing of direct sown vegetable crops (i.e. by seeds) for example Okra, Bitter gourd,
Harvesting Ridge gourd, Pumpkin can be done. Transplanting of 45 days old seedlings of vegetable crops (Brinjal, Tomato, Chilli etc.)
should be done at the places where rainfall was received by considering soil moisture. Harvesting of mature vegetable crops
should be done. Weed management should be done in vegetable crops.
Floriculture Transplanting/ Transplanting of Floriculture crops should be done at the places where rainfall was received by considering soil moisture.
Harvesting Harvesting of mature flowers should be done. Weed management should be done in floricultural crops.
Animal -- During the monsoon season, the prevalence of stable flies, mosquitoes, and ticks around livestock increases; therefore, as per
husbandry advice of veternary doctor spray the insecticides. Livestock should be vaccinated against monsoon-related diseases as
recommended by a veternary doctor. In particular, goats and sheep should be vaccinated against enterotoxaemia at the
appropriate time to prevent the disease during the monsoon. During monsoon season, avoid feeding livestock excessive amounts
of tender, green fodder.
